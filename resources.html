Business Owner FAQ

Questions worth answering before you buy.

What does Everest Journeys do?

Everest Journeys is a digital marketing, sales automation and business growth agency. It helps businesses generate leads, manage enquiries, follow up with customers and improve sales through connected digital systems.

Do you work with B2B and manufacturing companies?

Yes. The agency is positioned to support B2B and manufacturing businesses along with solar, automobile, travel, hospitality and other enquiry-driven businesses.

Do you only provide social media marketing?

No. Services include social media marketing, WhatsApp marketing, email marketing, Meta Ads, Google Business Profile, SEO, websites, CRM/lead management, content creation and complete sales funnel automation.

How much does the complete sales funnel cost?

The Complete Sales Funnel & Automation service starts at ₹24,999 one-time. More complex customization, multiple pipelines and additional integrations are priced according to scope.

Are software and API charges included?

Third-party costs such as CRM subscriptions, WhatsApp API/platform charges, IVR, email software, hosting, domain and advertising spend are paid separately by the client unless explicitly included.
